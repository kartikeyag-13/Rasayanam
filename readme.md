## The idea behind Rasayanam

The air around us is something we experience every moment, yet rarely stop to understand.

Across NCR, Punjab and Haryana, air pollution has become more than an environmental concern. It affects our everyday routines, our health and the way we live.

We wanted to build something that could make this information easier to understand and more meaningful to people.

That idea became **Rasayanam**.

Inspired by the Ayurvedic understanding of *Prakriti* (nature) and *Prana* (life force), Rasayanam brings environmental intelligence into a space that feels approachable, thoughtful and connected to nature.

It combines air-quality monitoring, machine learning, environmental insights and traditional wellbeing perspectives — with one simple intention:

**To help people understand the air they breathe and make more informed choices about their everyday wellbeing.**

## What you'll find here

- **The Air:** Explore current air-quality information and understand the conditions around supported locations.
- **AQI Map:** Discover regional air-quality patterns across NCR, Punjab and Haryana through an interactive map.
- **Insights:** Understand different pollutants and explore historical air-quality trends.
- **Forecast:** Explore upcoming air-quality conditions through experimental machine-learning predictions.
- **Health:** Learn about general precautions and how different pollution levels can affect everyday life.
- **Home Remedies:** Discover Ayurveda-inspired remedies, their ingredients, preparation and traditional context.
- **Explore:** Find additional information and resources related to air quality and wellbeing.

## Under the hood

Rasayanam is not just a collection of air-quality numbers. It brings together environmental data, data processing and predictive modelling to make those numbers more useful.

**Environmental data**

We use Open-Meteo Air Quality (CAMS) and weather data, alongside a historical dataset covering different locations across the region.

**Air-quality analysis**

Pollutant observations are processed into AQI indicators to help communicate environmental conditions.

**Machine learning**

Historical environmental patterns are used to develop forecasting models and explore how air quality may change over time.

**Visualisation**

Interactive maps, charts and a carefully designed interface help turn complex environmental information into something people can explore.

## A note on the data

Environmental data needs context.

CAMS provides model-based estimates, not direct readings from CPCB reference monitoring stations. Historical fallback data, cached observations and live data should be distinguished by their source and timestamp.

Machine-learning forecasts are experimental and depend on the availability and quality of input observations. They should not be treated as guaranteed predictions.

Similarly, the health and home-remedy sections are intended for general awareness and educational purposes, not as a substitute for professional medical advice or official pollution advisories.
