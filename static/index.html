An old philosophy. A new lens on the air.

Observe nature. Understand the air.

Air quality is more than a number. It is shaped by weather, place, time and human activity. Rasayanam brings those signals together, translating complex environmental data into useful everyday context. Its visual identity draws on Ayurveda's relationship with prakriti (nature), while its readings and forecasts remain grounded in data.
